My Process

Prototype before spec. I validate how a product should work — its flows, data, and edge cases — before a single requirement is locked.

Production Planning: BRD to Working Prototype

Full design cycle from a draft BRD to a three-screen interactive prototype — HO category mapping, outlet planning, and kitchen production entry — sharing one live data model. Went beyond the spec to encode system invariants: plans record intent without moving stock, unmapped items are blocked from planning, a cumulative stock-coverage guardrail flags shortage cascades across the horizon, and production entries are held (not lost) until goods receipt clears.
