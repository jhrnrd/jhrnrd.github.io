About Me

I've shipped across four distinct domains in under four years — logistics, maritime tech, govtech, and now enterprise ERP & platform. That range taught me how to enter complex industries fast, find the signal in stakeholder noise, and turn ambiguity into clear product direction.

Today I lead foundational platform work: defining core data models, system boundaries, and integration contracts that everything else is built on. I care about architecture that scales, auth flows that don't leak money, and squads that move with clarity.

I also use AI agents and LLM workflows to accelerate architecture design, documentation, and stakeholder alignment — not to replace rigor, but to compress the time between understanding and shipping.

My Process

How I take products from messy stakeholder needs to shipped reality.

Understand

Deep-dive into stakeholder needs, user research, and domain context. No shortcuts.

Define

Write the PRD. Clear user stories, acceptance criteria, metrics, and system boundaries.

Prototype

Lead cross-functional squads through sprint ceremonies. Measure, iterate, and scale.
